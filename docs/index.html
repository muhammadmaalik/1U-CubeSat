OPEN-SOURCE CUBESAT MISSION

SYSTEM-3

A 1U CubeSat built by students at Stony Brook University. Measuring gamma-ray background radiation from low-Earth orbit. All telemetry broadcast publicly.

Explore Mission ↓
Mission Profile

What We're Building

A fully open-source 1U CubeSat carrying a gamma-ray spectrometer payload. Every schematic, every line of code, every byte of telemetry — public and free.

🔬

Gamma-Ray Spectrometer

CsI(Tl) scintillator crystal paired with Hamamatsu SiPM arrays for radiation measurement in LEO.

📡

UHF Downlink

437.025 MHz AX.25 packet radio. Zero encryption. Receivable by anyone with a UHF antenna via SatNOGS.

⚡

Solar EPS

BQ25713 MPPT charger with dual LiFePO4 batteries. Autonomous power management for continuous operation.

🌐

100% Open Source

Hardware designs, firmware, ground station tools, and all science data — freely available under MIT license.

🛰️ Open Science Telemetry

All satellite data will be broadcast unencrypted on 437.025 MHz. Anyone on Earth can receive and decode our science packets. Data will be archived and published here in real-time.
