Mission Overview

Measuring cosmic radiation from orbit

SYSTEM-3 carries a miniaturized gamma-ray spectrometer — a CsI(Tl) scintillator crystal paired with a Hamamatsu SiPM array — into LEO to detect and catalog gamma photons in real-time. All telemetry is broadcast unencrypted through the global SatNOGS ground station network as open public science data.

Hardware

6-layer PCB stack architecture

Each 95 × 95 mm board in the stack serves a dedicated subsystem, from payload detection to antenna deployment.

Stage 1 — Payload
95.0 × 95.0 mm

CsI(Tl) Scintillator Crystal Sheet & Hamamatsu SiPM Array (C3015822)

Stage 2 — Analog Front-End
95.0 × 95.0 mm

Pico Spectrometer — LTC6227 Low-Noise Op-Amp with Noise Filtering Loop

Stage 3 — Flight Computer
95.0 × 95.0 mm

C&DH Core — STM32F411RET6 Microcontroller, EXTI10 Hardware Interrupt Mode

Stage 4 — Power
95.0 × 95.0 mm

EPS Solar Power Manager — TI BQ25713 Buck-Boost I2C MPPT Charger

Stage 5 — Energy Storage & Comms
95.0 × 95.0 mm

Dual 18650 LiFePO4 Batteries (2S1P) & RFM95W UHF Transceiver

Stage 6 — Antenna Deployment
95.0 × 95.0 mm

Passive Tape-Measure Dipole Elements & 10Ω 1W Burn-Wire Actuator
